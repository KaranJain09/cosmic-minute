TITLE:
NASA Found Mars Rocks That Can Trap Fossils 🔴 #Shorts

DESCRIPTION:
NASA's Perseverance rover found pale rocks at 'Idubi' in Jezero crater that are rich in hydrated silica (NASA update, Nov 16, 2026). Silica forms with water, and on Earth it preserves microscopic fossils for billions of years. Earlier silica finds were too small to study up close; these are big enough. No signs of life have been found.
