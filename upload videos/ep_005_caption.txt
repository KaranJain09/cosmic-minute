Jupiter: Earth's Bodyguard or Biggest Threat? 🪐 #Shorts

DESCRIPTION:
Light-speed journey, stop 5: Jupiter. About 35 light-minutes from Earth at closest. Around 1,300 Earths could fit inside, and it has more than twice the mass of all the other planets combined. Its gravity deflects some comets and redirects others toward the inner solar system. In 1994, Comet Shoemaker-Levy 9 struck it, leaving scars larger than Earth.
