TITLE:
The Asteroid Belt Is Almost Empty ☄️ #Shorts

DESCRIPTION:
Light-speed journey, stop 4: the asteroid belt. Its inner edge is about 10 light-minutes from Earth at closest. Asteroids average roughly a million kilometers apart, and spacecraft cross safely. The whole belt holds about 3% of the Moon's mass, around a third of it in the dwarf planet Ceres, known for bright salt deposits. Scientists think Jupiter's gravity kept a planet from forming here.
